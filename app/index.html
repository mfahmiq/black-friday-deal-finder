How we checked every deal

Each deal on this page was found and checked on 27 November 2026 by reading the price at the store it names, or in a deals roundup that names the store. Every card says when its price was read and where. The discount is always computed from the two prices shown — if they do not add up, the deal is wrong, and we would rather drop it than list it.

The verdict is our honest call. NEW LOW means the lowest price the cited source has ever tracked. STRONG DEAL is a deep cut on a current model, near its best price. GOOD DEAL is a real saving, but not the cheapest it has been. ROUTINE SALE is a small cut — worth it only if you need the thing now. We never list a product, store, price or link that no store or deals source showed when we checked it.
